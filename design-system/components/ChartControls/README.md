# ChartControls

The range picker above a price chart and the indicator toggles under it.

## When to use
With a holding's price chart (and the portfolio's value chart, which shares the range picker). Ranges: 1D, 1W, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, 2Y, 5Y, All. The API draws 2Y, 5Y and All in weekly bars. Every chart opens on 1D, each time its page opens.

1D is the latest trading day, not the last 24 hours (on a weekend those are empty), and it turns over at the regular open, not at midnight or 4 AM. Until the open it is the day before, as Apple Stocks draws it, so a morning's look shows yesterday's move. A market that never closes for the night (crypto, currencies through the week, futures, whose daily halt is an hour) has no trading day to keep: its 1D is the last 24 hours. Without that rule its last session would be the whole five-day fetch.

## What you provide
- The ranges and the selected one.
- The indicator toggles, and whether each is on:
  - "SMA bands": the 200-day average and the lines 5, 10 and 15% either side of it, on every range. When on, it shows how far the price sits from the average at the scrubbed or latest bar ("+4.6%").
  - "Earnings": a vertical line at each earnings report in the range.
- A note when Earnings doesn't apply to the range ("1W to 5Y"). SMA bands gets one when it is on but the price crosses no line, not even the average ("Stayed between the SMA and −5%"), and "Average unavailable" when the average couldn't be fetched after three tries (2s and 6s apart). Earnings gets one when it is on but draws no line: "No earnings reports" for a fund, crypto or currency, or "None in the past month" when no report falls in the range.

## Rules
- Ranges sit above the plot, as in Apple Stocks, packed to the left and 2px apart. Each is 34px wide where the row has room. Where it hasn't, every range narrows by the same amount: on a holding, the nine ranges and the outward arrow come to 33px a range at 393pt and 31px at 375pt. Each is 30px tall, radius 6px, `--ds-fs-11` 500 in `ds-text`. The chosen range is 700 on a `ds-line` pill, so the accent stays on the price line.
- The indicator chip: at least 30px tall, padding 0 10px, radius 6px, a 1px `ds-line` border, `--ds-fs-10` 500. Off: `ds-secondary` with a muted swatch. On: `ds-text`, with the swatch's three bars in the lines' colours as the chart stacks them: `ds-band-above`, the dashed `ds-text` average, then `ds-band-below`.
- The chip is its lines' legend: a 12px swatch, the name, then the value in mono.
- The average is always drawn. Of the bands, only those the price crosses in the range are drawn. Because the price moves continuously, the crossed bands are always one unbroken run from its low to its high. The average can stand apart from them, for example the SMA with +10% and +15% but no +5%.
- Only the lines the price crosses set the price scale, and that includes the average: when the price stays to one side of the average, the average never stretches the scale. Where it runs past the scale it is cut off at the plot's edge, and so are the tints beside it. When it ends past the scale, its name reads `SMA ↓` (or `SMA ↑`) and is held at that edge. Without this, a price 18% above its average would flatten a day's move into a line along the top.
- The average is read off at each bar. Within a session it holds still, so it is a level line on 1D and steps once a session on 1W and 1M.
- The average is a 1.5px dashed (4 3) `ds-text` line. Each band is a 1px line, `ds-band-above` over the average and `ds-band-below` under it, one colour a side. Every zone the price passes through is tinted in its outer line's colour, deeper the farther out: 5%, 9% and 13% for the ±5, ±10 and ±15% zones. A zone runs between its own two lines whether or not they are drawn, so a price that crosses only +15% still sits on the 10–15% zone's tint below that line, and one that crosses nothing sits on its zone's tint across the plot. A zone past the scale is cut off at the plot's edge. Past ±15% there is no zone. The tints replace the price's fill. When the price passes through no zone (the bands off, or the average still loading), the price keeps its fill.
- Each drawn line is named (`+15%` … `SMA` … `−15%`) just past its right end, in mono `--ds-fs-8` `ds-secondary`. The names get a column of their own between the plot and the price scale, 6px in from the plot's edge, so a name never covers the price. Where lines run closer than 11px, their names are spaced 11px apart about the lines' own centre.
- With the bands on, scrubbing replaces the one-line tip with a readout of every drawn line the crosshair crosses, listed top down in the order they cross it, with the price slotted in where it sits. Each row is a 10px swatch in the line's colour (dashed for the average), its name (`+15%` … `−15%` in mono, then `SMA`, `Price`), and its value in mono. The date heads the names and the currency code heads the values. The price's row is 700 in `ds-text`; the other names are `ds-secondary`. It uses the tip's box (`--ds-fs-9`, 1px `ds-line` border, radius 5px, `ds-bg`) and hangs 10px to whichever side of the crosshair has more room, so the crossings stay visible. Each crossing gets a 2.5px dot in its line's colour. The readout still lists an average that is off the scale, with its value; only its dot is cut off.
- Earnings: each report is a 1px solid `ds-secondary` line from the plot's top to its baseline, under the bands and the price, so it marks when without competing with what. It is darker than the `ds-line` time guides and solid where the scrub crosshair is dashed. Its chip's swatch is one upright 2 × 10px bar, `ds-muted` off and `ds-secondary` on. No label on the line: the chip is its legend.
- A report's line sits midway between the last bar before the report and the first bar after it, so it parts the closes struck before the news from those after. Companies report before the open or after the close, so the line falls in the overnight gap: on 1W and 1M, between one session's last bar and the next session's first. A weekly bar (2Y, 5Y) closes on Friday, so the line goes before the week the report fell in, or before the next week when the report came after Friday's close (Berkshire reports on Saturdays).
- Earnings draws on 1W to 5Y only. 1D shows one session, which a report never falls inside. All would put 40 lines 8px apart, a barcode the price can't be read through. 5Y's twenty lines, 14px apart, are as close as they get.
- Extended hours, always (a toggle until Nov 6, now gone: the pre-market and after hours are where the morning's and evening's news trades): the day is the latest trading day, from its pre-market's start (4 AM in New York) to its after-hours close (8 PM), as Yahoo marks them out, half-days included. It turns over at the regular open too: until then the chart is the day before, whole, with today's pre-market after its after hours, rather than a few thin hours of pre-market stretched across the plot. The night between them takes no width, and a guide named for the new day ("Fri") marks where it begins; that name is always kept, the hour labels beside it giving way. On the portfolio's chart the day starts where the current listings' days start, so a listing whose day ended hours before (Toronto on a Canadian holiday) doesn't pull it back. Bars sit at equal steps, as elsewhere, so the regular session takes under half the width.
- The stretches before the open and after the close sit on `ds-subtle`, from the plot's top to its baseline, each edge midway between the session's outermost bar and its neighbour. The price line runs through them in `ds-accent` at 40% opacity, with the regular session drawn over it at full strength. The fill lies under the regular session only. The latest-price dot stays full strength wherever it falls. With the bands on, their tints still span the whole day. A pre-market bar takes the average up to the session before, so the average steps once, at the open.
- On 1D, the latest price is tagged on the value scale at the line's end: a pill of `ds-accent` with the price in `ds-bg`, mono `--ds-fs-10` 600, 18px tall, radius 4px, 5px padding, right-aligned in the scale's column. The pill is held inside the plot's height. The column widens to fit it, 4px clear of the plot. A scale label it would touch gives way to it. The price is to its own precision, without the currency: whole above a thousand ("15,038"), cents above one ("350.88"), four places below ("0.9451"). It is the chart's last point, so after the close it is the latest price outside regular hours. On a holding or a watched symbol, the line's end and the tag follow the price as it streams in: every trade, those outside the regular session too, overnight included. A trade within the last bar's five minutes moves that bar. A later trade is drawn as a point of its own, and five minutes later the bars are fetched again. Overnight trades (8 PM to 4 AM) are not refetched, because Yahoo's chart has no overnight bars: the latest overnight trade is the one point past 8 PM, in the after-hours shading. The portfolio's chart updates when its page opens or is pulled to refresh. The tag stays on while scrubbing. Other ranges have no tag.
- A scrub on an extended bar adds its session to the time: "5:10 PM · After hours", "8:55 AM · Pre-market", "9:34 PM · Overnight". So does the portfolio's readout. On a 1D that runs over two dates, the time takes its day: "Thu 3:55 PM", "Fri 7:20 AM · Pre-market".
- The figures around the chart stay the regular session's: a holding's price and Today, the portfolio's total and its 1D change. The chart can end past them, at the latest trade outside regular hours, overnight included. On the portfolio's chart, each holding counts at its latest trade, and a listing that hasn't traded yet that day counts at its previous close.
- A disabled chip (Earnings on 1D or All) drops to 45% opacity. A chip's notes sit beside it in `ds-muted`; each chip and its notes wrap together, so a note never strays to the other chip.
- The plot itself: a vertical swipe scrolls the page, a horizontal one scrubs.

Static rendition, hand-written from `packages/portifo-web/src/index.css` (`.hd-chart-controls`, `.hd-range`, `.hd-indicator*`; the scrub readout is `.hd-chart-ladder`, and the chart's lines are not previewed) and `src/components/HoldingChart.tsx`.
