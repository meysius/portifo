The hierarchy 01 — 03
01 / THE CONTEXT

Find the right move.

Symbol, type and account filters sit directly above the ledger. The record count keeps the full timeline in context.

02 / THE RECORD

Four moves. One timeline.

Explicit labels and quiet, directional icons separate buys, sells, deposits and withdrawals. Signed amounts show the cash impact, not profit or loss.

03 / THE GUIDANCE

A clear path, screen by screen.

A full-page wizard gives each step room. Previous answers are tappable, and a Today chip opens a wheel-style date sheet. Review before recording. Set currency balances to turn each difference into a deposit or withdrawal, without converting currencies.
