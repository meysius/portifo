The hierarchy 01 — 03
01 / YOUR MONEY

Value, not the ticker price.

The biggest number answers “what is my position worth?” Overall gain sits directly underneath, with colour reserved for the return.

02 / THE CONTEXT

Your return. The market’s price.

Today’s position return belongs with your holding value. Share price leads the market section and its history chart—even after you’ve sold every share.

03 / THE OWNERSHIP

Depth, without repetition.

A compact position ledger leads into accounts. Expand an account in place; tap a purchase to push its transaction detail. No competing destinations.
