The hierarchy 01 — 03
01 / THE PLACES

Your accounts, not another dashboard.

One generic account icon, the name, and its total USD value. The same compact spacing and type sizes as the transactions ledger, without content labels or a repeated portfolio dashboard.

02 / THE CONTENTS

One level deeper.

An account pushes onto the navigation stack. Its balance leads into investments and cash, then the familiar holdings ledger. Cash keeps its native currency.

03 / THE BEGINNING

A name is enough.

Add an account without connecting a broker. Opening cash is optional, with room for more than one currency. A focused full-screen form keeps the task simple.
