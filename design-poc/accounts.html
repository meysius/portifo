The hierarchy 01 — 03
01 / THE IDENTITY

Straight to your accounts.

The page title leads directly into the list. Account names and institutions provide context without an extra summary or toolbar.

02 / THE ACCOUNTS

A list, not a wall of cards.

Account name and institution come first. A clear balance, cash amount and quiet mix bar follow. Flat rows and hairline dividers keep the view calm, even as the list grows.

03 / THE CONTROL

A home you can make your own.

Tap a row for its breakdown and edit its details, or add a manually tracked account without connecting a brokerage.
