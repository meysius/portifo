The hierarchy 01 — 03
01 / THE WHOLE

Your money, together.

Cash and investments add up to one quiet headline. Today’s move sits underneath, without colouring the balance itself.

02 / THE JOURNEY

Growth, with context.

A cobalt value chart tracks the portfolio over time. The period’s value change includes deposits; the detail sheet separates those flows from investment gains.

03 / THE PARTS

From balance to breakdown.

Stocks and cash show the broad allocation. Today’s movers chart up to five of the strongest daily percentage changes around a shared zero line. In the holdings ledger, each coloured ring traces its share of the whole portfolio, with the exact percentage in the centre, alongside shares, value and returns.
