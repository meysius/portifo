The hierarchy 01 — 03
01 / THE WHOLE

Your money, together.

Cash and investments add up to one quiet headline. Today’s move sits underneath, without colouring the balance itself.

02 / THE JOURNEY

Growth, with context.

A cobalt value chart tracks the portfolio over time. The period’s value change includes deposits; the detail sheet separates those flows from investment gains.

03 / THE PARTS

From balance to breakdown.

Stocks and cash lead into a colourful stock breakdown. Today’s movers chart up to five of the strongest daily percentage changes around a shared zero line. The holdings ledger stays focused on shares, value and returns, without repeating weights.
